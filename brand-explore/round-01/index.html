U3zub — Logo Round 01

12 directional sketches. Black/white only. Each tile shows the same SVG on light + dark to preview reversibility.

01 · Stencil military
Heavy block letters with horizontal stencil bridges. Reads as banner / military signage.
02 · Brutalist slab
Tight ultra-bold lockup with a structural underbar. Concrete-and-steel weight.
03 · Constructivist skew
Forward-leaning letters between two posts — agitprop poster lineage.
04 · Blackletter folk
Gothic blackletter with twin underline rule. Ancestral / heraldic tone.
05 · Cyrillic blend
Letterforms borrow from У/З/У/Б — reads bilingually. Decode line below.
06 · Trident ligature
The "3" replaced by a literal trident whose body curves into the digit shape.
07 · Carved petroglyph
Slightly tilted serif letters with chip marks — chiseled-into-stone feel.
08 · Vyshyvanka pixel
Pixel-grid letters echoing cross-stitch with diamond border ornaments.
09 · Bunker negative
Solid black slab; letters are cut-outs. Maximum mass, minimum decoration.
10 · Compressed tall
Ultra-narrow stretched letters. Soviet-poster vertical drama.
11 · Sigil monogram
Trident-into-U glyph stacked above the wordmark. Lockup, not a separate mark.
12 · Newspaper defiant
Heavy serif framed by double rules. Reads like a samizdat front page.
